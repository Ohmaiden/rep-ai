Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't the app counting my reps?

Make sure your full body is visible in the camera frame and you're in a well-lit space. The camera needs a clear view to detect your movement accurately.

Which exercises are supported?

Rep AI currently supports push-ups. More exercises are coming in future updates.

The app is asking for camera permission — is that safe?

Yes. The camera is used only for real-time rep counting on your device. No video is recorded or sent anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I reset my workout history?

Go to the Settings screen inside the app and tap "Clear History".

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Email us at omar.a-c27@hotmail.com with a description of the issue and your device model. We'll fix it ASAP.
